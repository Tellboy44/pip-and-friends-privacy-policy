Privacy Policy

Pip & Friends Playground · Burley Builds · Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how Pip & Friends Playground handles information. The games are designed for young children and are intended to be used with a parent or guardian.

Information used in the games

Game progress, language choices, and play settings are stored on the device. The app does not require a player account. The games do not use advertising or advertising identifiers, and do not request location, contacts, or photos. The app does not include third-party analytics in the current release.

If a device's text-to-speech service is used for a line without a recorded voice, that service is provided by the device platform and is subject to that provider's own privacy terms.

Amazon subscription purchases

If subscription purchasing is offered in a release, Amazon Appstore processes the purchase, payment, renewal, and cancellation. To verify a purchase and restore access, the app sends the Amazon account's app-specific user ID and purchase receipt ID over HTTPS to Burley Builds' receipt-verification service. That service asks Amazon's Receipt Verification Service to validate the receipt and returns whether the subscription is active. The app does not receive or store payment-card details.

The receipt-verification service is designed to check receipts without maintaining a player profile or purchase database. Amazon and the cloud hosting provider process information as needed to provide and secure their services and may retain their own operational or transaction records under their respective policies.

Who processes information

  • Amazon Appstore: handles purchases and subscription management. See Amazon's privacy notice.
  • Amazon Receipt Verification Service: checks the validity and status of Amazon purchase receipts.
  • AWS: hosts the receipt-verification service when it is deployed. See AWS's privacy notice.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this policy page and may process ordinary website request and security information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Choices for parents and guardians

Game progress and settings can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Amazon subscriptions must be managed or cancelled through the Amazon account used to purchase them. Removing the app does not cancel an Amazon subscription. Amazon may retain transaction records required to operate its store and meet its obligations.

Children's privacy

The games are designed for young children. We do not ask children to provide names, email addresses, or other direct contact details to play. Subscription actions are intended for a parent or guardian and are presented behind a grown-ups' gate. Parents or guardians can contact us with questions or requests concerning information handled by Burley Builds.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The latest version and its effective date will be published on this page.
